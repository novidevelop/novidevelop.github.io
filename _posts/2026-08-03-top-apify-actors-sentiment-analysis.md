# Top Apify Actors for Sentiment Analysis in 2026: Reviews, Social Media & AI Tools

Sentiment analysis has evolved from a nice-to-have data science experiment into a core operational strategy for brands, marketers, and researchers. By harvesting user-generated content from across the web—whether customer reviews, social media comments, or news articles—organizations can turn raw public opinions into actionable intelligence.

To power these AI pipelines, [Apify](https://apify.com/use-cases/sentiment-analysis?fpr=7hce1m) offers a marketplace of specialized serverless web scrapers known as **Actors**. In this article, we review the top Apify Actors for sentiment analysis, grouped by their primary data sources, and highlight three essential guidebooks across our developer blog ecosystem to help you build automated sentiment pipelines.

---

## 1. Review Scrapers: Capturing Voice of Customer (VoC)

Customer feedback on e-commerce and review platforms provides direct insights into product quality, service bottlenecks, and brand perception.

### 🌟 Google Maps Reviews Scraper ([`compass/google-maps-reviews-scraper`](https://apify.com/compass/google-maps-reviews-scraper?fpr=7hce1m))
- **Primary Use Case:** Local business sentiment, hospitality feedback, and regional customer satisfaction.
- **Key Features:** Extracts review text, star ratings, response timestamps, owner replies, and reviewer profile details.
- **Why it Matters:** Allows local businesses and franchise operators to track shifting customer sentiment over time and identify specific locations needing operational improvements.

### 🌟 Amazon Reviews Scraper ([`junglee/amazon-reviews-scraper`](https://apify.com/junglee/amazon-reviews-scraper?fpr=7hce1m))
- **Primary Use Case:** E-commerce product feedback and competitive analysis.
- **Key Features:** Scrapes full text reviews, star ratings, verified purchase badges, review images, and helpfulness votes.
- **Why it Matters:** Invaluable for D2C brands analyzing customer complaints, feature requests, or benchmarking against rival product listings.

### 🌟 Facebook & Booking.com Review Scrapers
- **Facebook Reviews Scraper ([`apify/facebook-reviews-scraper`](https://apify.com/apify/facebook-reviews-scraper?fpr=7hce1m)):** Captures customer reviews on business Facebook Pages.
- **Booking Reviews Scraper ([`voyager/booking-reviews-scraper`](https://apify.com/voyager/booking-reviews-scraper?fpr=7hce1m)):** Extracts detailed guest feedback, including liked/disliked pros & cons, room details, and stay dates.

## 2. Social Media Scrapers: Tracking Brand Sentiment & Virality

Social media channels offer real-time sentiment data, revealing viral trends, audience engagement, and emerging crisis signals.

### 🌟 Fast TikTok Scraper & Comment Scraper ([`xtdata/tiktok-scraper`](https://apify.com/xtdata/tiktok-scraper?fpr=7hce1m) & [`xtdata/tiktok-comment-scraper`](https://apify.com/xtdata/tiktok-comment-scraper?fpr=7hce1m))
- **Primary Use Case:** Short-form video comment sentiment, viral campaign tracking, and audience feedback analysis.
- **Key Features:** High-speed pay-per-result data extraction, video metadata, captions, top comments, nested comment reply chains, and user profile information without complex proxy setup.
- **Why it Matters:** Built by the Novi / xtdata team, these Actors are optimized for high-reliability scraping at a fraction of the cost, making them ideal for monitoring how audiences react to product launches and influencer campaigns.

### 🌟 X.com (Twitter) API Scraper ([`xtdata/twitter-x-scraper`](https://apify.com/xtdata/twitter-x-scraper?fpr=7hce1m))
- **Primary Use Case:** Real-time brand monitoring, crisis detection, and public sentiment analysis on X.com.
- **Key Features:** No-code solution to extract historical tweets, replies, user profiles, and keyword/hashtag search results with advanced filtering (Top, Latest, Media).
- **Why it Matters:** At just ~$0.50 per 1,000 tweets, this xtdata Actor provides an extremely cost-effective way for market researchers and PR teams to extract X.com sentiment data seamlessly.

### 🌟 All-in-One Social Media Sentiment Tool ([`tri_angle/social-media-sentiment-analysis-tool`](https://apify.com/tri_angle/social-media-sentiment-analysis-tool?fpr=7hce1m))
- **Primary Use Case:** Multi-platform comment scraping with integrated AI sentiment scoring.
- **Key Features:** Enter a profile handle, and the Actor automatically scrapes recent posts/comments across Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, providing sentiment polarity (positive, neutral, negative) out of the box.

## 3. Market Research & AI Extractor Actors

For broader market research and AI model enrichment, raw web content must be structured and scored.

- **Website Content Crawler ([`apify/website-content-crawler`](https://apify.com/apify/website-content-crawler?fpr=7hce1m)):** Crawls websites and converts pages to clean Markdown to feed RAG and LLM sentiment classification pipelines.
- **AI Text Analyzer for Google Reviews ([`geneea-analytics/reviews-text-nlp-analyzer`](https://apify.com/geneea-analytics/reviews-text-nlp-analyzer?fpr=7hce1m)):** Applies natural language processing directly to Google Maps reviews to extract recurring topic keywords and sentiment distribution.
- **Sentiment Analysis Online Tool ([`tri_angle/sentiment-analysis-online-tool`](https://apify.com/tri_angle/sentiment-analysis-online-tool?fpr=7hce1m)):** AI-powered Actor that processes batches of text input and returns sentiment scores (0 to 1) with detailed classifications.
